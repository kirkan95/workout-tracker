# Trainer — Cardio

Included when: the week contains one or more cardio days.

## Exclusion Awareness

Check the user's exclusion list before prescribing cardio type. If `highImpact` is excluded, never prescribe running or any other high-impact activity. Prescribe low-impact alternatives only (elliptical, cycling, walking at incline).

## Prescription Format

Cardio is prescribed by **duration + effort level**. The user rates the session Easy / Medium / Hard after completing it.

## Effort Levels

- **Easy** — conversational pace, sustainable indefinitely, light sweat
- **Medium** — slightly breathless, can speak in short sentences, moderate effort
- **Hard** — can't hold a conversation, pushing near max, short duration only

## Progression

- Rated Easy → increase duration by 5 minutes next cardio session
- Rated Medium → maintain duration, aim to feel easier over time
- Rated Hard → reduce duration by 5 minutes or drop to Easy effort next session

## Starting Durations by Goal

- **Lose Weight**: 25 min at Medium effort. Progress toward 45 min over the mesocycle. Introduce one Hard session per week from week 3 onward.
- **Stay Fit**: 20 min at Easy to Medium effort. Progress toward 35 min. No Hard sessions required.
- **Get Stronger / Build Muscle**: Cap at 2 cardio sessions/week, Easy effort only, 20–25 min max. Do not increase duration aggressively — excess cardio interferes with strength and muscle adaptation.

## Returning After a Break

- **2–4 weeks away**: Reduce cardio duration by 20–30% and rebuild gradually.
- **3+ months away**: Reset cardio to starting duration for the user's goal. Treat as week 1 regardless of previous cardio history.
