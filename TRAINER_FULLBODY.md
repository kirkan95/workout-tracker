# Trainer — Full Body

Included for: **Lose Weight**, **Stay Fit**

## Rep Ranges

- **Lose Weight**: 12–20 reps. Moderate weight, short rest (30–45 sec). Keeping heart rate elevated matters as much as load.
- **Stay Fit**: 10–15 reps. Moderate weight, moderate rest (60 sec). Focus on consistency and full range of motion over max effort.

## Volume

Full body sessions hit all major movement patterns in one workout rather than isolating muscle groups. Scale sets per movement pattern to the user's target workout duration:

- **Under 30 min**: 1–2 sets per pattern (5–10 total sets)
- **30–45 min**: 2–3 sets per pattern (10–15 total sets)
- **45+ min**: 3 sets per pattern (15 total sets)

Fatigue accumulates fast when training the whole body — do not exceed 3 sets per movement pattern. Prioritize filling all 5 movement slots before adding extra sets to any single one.

Mesocycle deload still applies every 4–6 weeks — reduce sets by ~40% for one week, keep the same weight.

## Session Structure

Each full body session covers one exercise per movement pattern in this order:
1. **Squat** — quad and glute dominant
2. **Hinge** — hamstring and glute dominant
3. **Push** — chest, shoulders, triceps
4. **Pull** — back, biceps
5. **Core** — always last

Optional 6th slot: **Carry** — loaded carries, farmer's walks. Add when time allows.

Prioritize compound movements. Isolation work is low priority for both goals.

## Exercise Rotation

**Lose Weight**
Variety aids adherence and calorie burn matters more than movement specificity. Rotate exercises freely — weekly or bi-weekly. Prioritize metabolic and full body compound movements. Keep sessions feeling fresh to maintain motivation.

**Stay Fit**
Adherence is the primary goal. Rotate every 3–4 weeks — enough consistency to make measurable progress, enough variety to prevent boredom. Compound movements only; skip isolation work unless a movement pattern slot cannot be filled otherwise.
